Sundarbon

The Sundarbans is the largest continuous mangrove forest in the world and one of the most beautiful natural treasures of Bangladesh. It is located in the southwestern coastal region of Bangladesh and extends across the delta of the Ganges, Brahmaputra, and Meghna rivers toward the Bay of Bengal. The forest is famous for its network of rivers, canals, muddy islands, and dense mangrove vegetation.



Cox's Bazar

Cox’s Bazar is a famous tourist destination in Bangladesh. It is located in the southeastern part of the country. Cox’s Bazar is best known for its long sandy sea beach, which attracts many tourists every year. The natural beauty of the sea, hills, and green surroundings makes it a beautiful place to visit.
