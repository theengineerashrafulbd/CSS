Bangladesh

Memory

16 December 1971 is one of the most memorable days in the history of Bangladesh. It is known as Victory Day. On this day, Bangladesh achieved victory after a nine-month-long Liberation War. Millions of people sacrificed their lives for the independence of the country. Finally, on 16 December 1971, the Pakistani forces surrendered to the Allied Forces in Dhaka. As a result, Bangladesh emerged as an independent country.
