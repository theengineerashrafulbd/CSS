City University

City University is one of the leading private universities in Bangladesh. It was established in 2002 with the goal of providing quality higher education. The university offers modern learning facilities and experienced faculty members.

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various disciplines, including Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Business Administration (BBA), Civil Engineering, Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE), Law, English, and Pharmacy.

City University focuses on practical learning, research, and innovation. Students gain both theoretical knowledge and hands-on experience through laboratories, projects, seminars, and workshops to prepare for their future careers.

The university provides a friendly and supportive learning environment. Students can participate in different clubs, cultural events, sports competitions, and leadership activities that help develop their communication and teamwork skills.

City University is committed to producing skilled, responsible, and ethical graduates. With a strong emphasis on academic excellence and professional development, it helps students become successful in both national and international careers.
