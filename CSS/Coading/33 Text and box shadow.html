Space known as outer space, is the vast and seemingly endless region that exists beyond Earth's atmosphere. It is a mysterious and fascinating place filled with billions of stars, planets, moons, asteroids, comets, galaxies, and other celestial objects. Unlike Earth, space is almost a perfect vacuum, meaning it contains very little matter and no breathable air. Because there is no atmosphere in space, sound cannot travel, temperatures can become extremely hot or cold depending on exposure to the Sun, and gravity is much weaker than on Earth.
